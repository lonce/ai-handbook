# Galgo AI Handbook

**Audience:** All staff, including leadership, documentation and quality teams, and developers.

**Status:** Initial draft for review; informational, not approved Galgo policy.

**Page type:** Explanation.

This handbook helps Galgo colleagues understand AI models and agents, assess their usefulness, and develop repeatable ways to use them at work. It brings shared explanations and practical guidance together so that each reader does not have to start from scratch.

## Where to start

The handbook is just beginning. These reader paths describe the material planned as it grows:

| Reader path | Questions the handbook will help answer |
| --- | --- |
| Leadership | Where could AI help, what would it cost, and how should a useful pilot be evaluated? |
| Documentation and quality teams | How can AI help with information and drafting tasks while preserving sources and human review? |
| Developers and technical staff | How do coding agents, tools, instructions, testing, and review fit together? |
| Everyone | What are models and agents, where do they run, and how should we check their output? |

## Scope and boundaries

This is a general AI learning and practice resource for Galgo. Controlled documents, procedures, and records are managed separately through Dot Compliance. Project-specific software documentation belongs in the relevant product repository.

Suggestions and experiments need clear status labels. A proposal, a vendor claim, a result tested by Galgo, and an approved practice provide different kinds of evidence. Changing information, such as model availability and prices, needs dated sources.

The handbook does not establish clinical, regulatory, legal, privacy, or security decisions on Galgo's behalf. Those questions need the appropriate human owner. Examples will be hypothetical or sanitized unless their use has been explicitly authorized.
